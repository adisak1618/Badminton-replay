# badminton-scoreboard

A browser-only app for filming casual badminton matches with a live scoreboard.

Two phones pair peer-to-peer: one at the back corner of the court records video,
the other sits at the net post as a large tappable scoreboard. Every point is
logged with its time in the recording so the score can be burned into the video
during or after the match.

Status: design phase. See `docs/` for requirements, UX design and technical design.
